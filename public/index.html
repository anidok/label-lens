FSSAI 2022 Draft Regulatory Framework

Label Lens directly implements the mathematical criteria published by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) under the Draft Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Amendment Regulations, 2022.

🍬 Total Sugars Criterion

Threshold: Energy from sugars must be ≤ 10% of total calories.

Sugar Energy % = (Sugars in g × 4 kcal) ÷ Total Energy × 100

🧈 Saturated Fat Criterion

Threshold: Energy from saturated fat must be ≤ 10% of total calories.

Sat Fat Energy % = (Sat Fat in g × 9 kcal) ÷ Total Energy × 100

🧂 Sodium Density Criterion

Threshold: Sodium must be ≤ 1.0 mg per 1.0 kcal of total food energy.

Sodium Ratio = Sodium in mg ÷ Total Energy in kcal

Heuristic Penalty Scoring (1.0 to 5.0)

Starts at 5.0 points and systematically applies deductions for verified health concerns:

  • -1.0 point for each nutrient (Sugar, Saturated Fat, Sodium) that exceeds the FSSAI HFSS draft threshold.
  • -0.5 point for borderline nutrients (within regulatory limit but elevated).
  • -0.5 point compound penalty if the product is classified as overall HFSS.
  • -0.5 point per flagged high-concern synthetic additive (capped at -1.5 points).
  • Score floored at 1.0 to avoid negative ratings.
